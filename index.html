Waffle Workshop

Build it. Cook it. Eat it

Choose Your Waffle:

Waffles are literally the best breakfast food ever because they are basically pancakes but with built-in pockets. You pour the batter into a hot waffle iron, press it down, and it cooks into this golden, crispy square covered in tiny boxes. Those little squares are perfect because they trap all the melted butter and maple syrup so it doesn't spill everywhere. They are super crunchy on the outside but really soft and fluffy when you bite into them. Plus, you can eat them with whipped cream and strawberries for dessert, or even with fried chicken, which sounds weird but tastes amazing.
